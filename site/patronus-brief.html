Drought drains hydropower reservoirs, and then the lights go out. NASA's data sees it coming months ahead. Patronus turns that signal into a forecast, a cost, and a decision.

01 · The problemIt keeps happening

The warning was there.Nobody acted on it.

Five hydro crises on four continents in four years. Each one built up slowly in rain, soil and reservoir data before the blackouts started.

02 · The ideaTrend, then timing, then cost

Three questions,one product.

Other teams will stop at a trend globe. We answer the challenge with the trend, then go two steps further.

Layer 1 · Trend Is this basin drying out?

Mann-Kendall, Sen's slope and FDR on 2002 to 2026 GRACE water storage and GPM rainfall for every major hydropower basin.

Answers the challenge: what, where, how much, is it significant
Layer 2 · Forecast When will the lights go out?

Probability that hydro falls below a danger threshold in each of the next 6 months, with calibrated ranges.

The "months to darkness" gauge
Layer 3 · Decision What does waiting cost?

Shortfall in MWh times replacement cost, plus blackout hours. Compare acting now with acting later.

Our differentiator

06 · Technology directionsOptions, not decided

Cool tech thatearns its place.

Each one must help us see earlier, decide better, explain clearly or prove it. Each plugs into the spine and has a fallback.

Jev, TypeSafe's System One modelDecide · Explain · Prove

A fast model that takes messy text or program state and returns typed decisions with calibrated probabilities. TypeSafe reports 70 to 500 ms per call and $0.042 per million input tokens. It's a decision layer, not a forecaster, so it runs on top of our validated model.

Read the world's newsMap-reduce millions of GDELT articles into typed events: {country, type: load_shedding | rationing | tariff, date}. Builds every crisis timeline automatically.
Call the alert levelTurn forecast plus context into watch | warning | act with a confidence, as a "smart if" in the alert feed.
Check the AI briefScore each sentence of the written brief as supported or unsupported by the data before it ships.
Adds
Speed and scale for news and alerts, and a standout piece of new tech.
Risk
Early access waitlist, so confirm we have a key today. Speed and cost numbers are TypeSafe's own.
Every reservoir, measured from spaceSee

A segmentation foundation model (SAM2, Prithvi-EO-2.0 or TerraMind) outlines reservoirs in Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1 imagery. SWOT heights turn area into volume.

Adds
A live watchlist of hundreds of reservoirs, not a handful.
Risk
SWOT starts in 2023; before that, area only. Run it in Earth Engine.
JEPA Earth world modelPredict

Learns how monthly NASA fields evolve in a latent space. A NeurIPS 2026 model, WeatherGenerator, uses the same idea. Its surprise is an alarm; its embeddings find analogs.

Adds
"The Zambezi today looks like October 2023, five months before the blackouts."
Risk
Needs a GPU and must beat LightGBM on the scoreboard. Fallback: analogs on raw anomalies.
Season scenariosPredict

NASA GEOS-S2S ensemble as the headline forecast. Optionally, Ai2's ACE2 emulator forced with El Niño ocean temperatures for hundreds of cheap scenarios.

Adds
Real probability spread for the coming rainy season.
Risk
ACE2 doesn't beat physics models yet, so use it for spread only.
Reservoir decision engineDecide

A water-balance simulator plus stochastic optimization over the scenarios. Brazil's grid operator plans with this family of methods. Conformal prediction keeps ranges honest.

Adds
A recommended rationing schedule and a cost-of-waiting curve.
Risk
Release records are thin; physics handles actions.
AI analyst and MCP serverExplain

A Claude agent that runs what-ifs with real tools and writes the brief in Bemba, Shona, Portuguese or Spanish, with every number linked to its dataset. Patronus is also exposed as an MCP server.

Adds
Decision-makers can ask questions in their own language.
Risk
Low. Pair with the Jev claim check.
Proof layerProve

Automatic blackout detection from daily Black Marble night lights. Each live forecast timestamped cryptographically, so "we called it on Nov 15" is provable.

Adds
Objective outcomes and forecasts nobody can quietly edit.
Risk
Rolling blackouts are noisy at night-light resolution.
The showExplain

A 3D globe (CesiumJS or deck.gl), Kariba in 3D terrain with the water line dropping, a night-lights time-lapse, and a scrolling story page that is the pitch.

Adds
The Presentation score and the demo video.
Risk
None to the science. Freeze features by Sunday 3 PM.
