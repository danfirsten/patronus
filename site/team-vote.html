01 · The three ideasWhat each one actually is
PatronusConcept

Forecasts the chance that drought drains a country's hydropower in each of the next 6 months, and what waiting will cost. Global trend map, crisis scoreboard, 2026 watchlist, and a Kariba close-up with a dated public forecast.

Hook: Ecuador lost about 1 to 1.5% of GDP in 2024. El Niño is back, and Kariba's 2027 power allocation is already cut by 36%.

Future DetectorWorking app

Weekly wildfire risk for every 10 km of California from about a dozen data layers, tested on 2023 to 2025 fires it never saw. Has look-alike past cases, a hashed prediction ledger, a Proof page, and side tests on floods, landslides and Africa food security.

Its own results: 1.6× better ranking than climatology. 32% of 1,450 fires flagged ahead vs 22% for climatology. NASA layers add no reliable gain over fire history alone. The Africa alarm and the Oregon/Washington transfer did not work.

HarvestShieldConcept

Finds crop stress that greenness hides, using fluorescence (OCO-2 SIF) and thermal data (ECOSTRESS) with a pretrained greenness forecaster. Then stress-tests a buyer's sourcing regions against drought scenarios.

Its own caveats: outputs are vegetation stress, not tonnes or dollars. The portfolio is fictional, the model hasn't been run, and novelty is unproven.

The deciding point

Fire starts are random, so slow satellite signals barely help. Reservoirs fill and drain over months, and GRACE, SWOT and SMAP measure exactly that water. The physics favors NASA data in Patronus.

Bias check: Claude helped develop Patronus, so weigh this with that in mind. Patronus is scored on what it could become and on what it still has to prove.

03 · Claude's recommendationOption A below
Patronus is the product and the story

Hydropower blackout early warning with the cost of waiting.

Future Detector is the engine

Its pipeline, held-out tests, confidence intervals, ablations, look-alike cases, ledger and Proof page, switched from fire cells to river basins. About half of Patronus, already built.

HarvestShield's best idea becomes a feature

"Your backup grid is in the same drought." Colombia cut power exports to Ecuador in Oct 2024 during its own drought; Zambia and Zimbabwe share Kariba.

One closing line for range

"The same engine tested wildfire, floods and food security. Here's where it worked and where it didn't."

Lesson to carry over

Future Detector's Africa alarm failed because it rested on one drought. Patronus's five-crisis scoreboard has the same risk, so the main test uses many country-months of hydro data, with the crises as illustrations.

Check in the first hour

Does the pipeline run on monthly basin data? Are Kariba lake levels and Ember coverage available? Is the Future Detector team happy to make their engine the backbone?
