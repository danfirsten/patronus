# Peru: brief for decision-makers
*Issued 15 Nov 2026. AI-written brief, checked against the Patronus data files (country/PER.json, enso.json). Not official advice.*

**Bottom line.** Patronus rates Peru's short-range hydro risk as **low** (highest monthly chance of a shortfall 13%). However, Peru is one of the grids that lose under El Niño, and that is the risk to watch over the 2027 dry season.

**What the data show**
- Peru's hydro basins have historically received **7% less rain** in El Niño water years (p = 0.04), and 12% less in the five strongest El Niños.
- Rain near the hydro plants (GPCP) was close to normal over the last three months and 8% below normal over the last twelve.
- Water storage (GRACE-FO, August 2026) is slightly below normal (−0.9 standard deviations).

**Suggested focus.** The risk builds over the 2027 dry season. Track reservoir levels from April onwards, against the El Niño outlook.

**Limits.** Monthly country forecasts have little skill beyond one month, and El Niño affects Peru's coast and Andes differently. See the Scoreboard and Proof pages.
