# Viet Nam: brief for decision-makers
*Issued 15 Nov 2026. AI-written brief, checked against the Patronus data files (country/VNM.json, enso.json). Not official advice.*

**Bottom line.** Patronus rates Viet Nam at **watch**. The highest monthly chance of a hydro shortfall from December 2026 to May 2027 is 25%. The main reason for concern is El Niño, not today's water levels.

**What the data show**
- Viet Nam's hydro basins have historically received **9% less rain** in El Niño water years (GPCP, 1981–2025, 16 El Niño years, p < 0.01). A strong El Niño is forecast through early 2027, so the window to watch is October 2026 to September 2027.
- Water storage near the hydro plants (GRACE-FO, August 2026) is 1.7 standard deviations above normal. That is a buffer, which is why the short-range risk is only moderate.
- In the 2023 crisis, El Niño was developing, about 900 GWh of demand went unserved, and the World Bank's preliminary estimate of the cost was about $1.4 billion (0.3% of GDP). Patronus' country model raised an alarm in November 2022, 6 months before onset. It was the only one of six crises the country model caught.

**Suggested focus.** Watch reservoir drawdown in the north during the 2027 dry season (March–June), and plan northern imports and demand response early.

**Limits.** Monthly country forecasts from power data have little skill beyond one month (see Scoreboard). The El Niño signal is the more reliable early indicator here.
