# Ecuador: brief for decision-makers
*Issued 15 Nov 2026. AI-written brief, checked against the Patronus data files (country/ECU.json, enso.json, crises.json). Not official advice.*

**Bottom line.** Patronus rates Ecuador's short-range hydro risk as **low** (highest monthly chance of a shortfall 14%). Hydro output in August 2026 was 14% above normal for the month.

**What the data show**
- Water storage near the hydro plants (GRACE-FO, August 2026) is 1.1 standard deviations below normal, and rain has been 12–16% below normal over the last 3–6 months. Basins have been unusually hot over the last 3 months (+2.4 SD).
- In Ecuador's record there is **no reliable El Niño signal** for hydro rainfall, so the 0–6 month water layer matters most.
- The 2023–24 crisis cost an estimated 1–1.5% of GDP (finance minister). It peaked at 14 hours a day without power from 24 October 2024. The grid operator CENACE warned repeatedly from October 2023. Patronus' monthly country model missed all three waves, which is a limit we report openly.

**Suggested focus.** Ecuador's Andean basins are smaller than GRACE's ~300 km footprint. Local reservoir data (Mazar, Paute) should complement the satellite view.
