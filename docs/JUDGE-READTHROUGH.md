# Judge read-through (Nov 14, 2026)

I read both live sites the way a Space Apps judge would and checked them against `DESIGN.md` §12 (source of truth), §8 (verified wordings) and `docs/CHALLENGE.md`. No `FACTS.md` exists in the repo or in open PRs #1–#3.

- **Official:** https://patronus-blackout-watch.aui-svi.workers.dev (home = "Story")
- **Preview (PR #3):** https://patronus-preview.aui-svi.workers.dev (home = new pitch landing, story moved to `#/story`)

**How I checked:** headless Chrome (Playwright) on every route at 1440×900 and 390×844, with console and network capture, axe-core (WCAG 2 A/AA), a keyboard Tab walk, a status check on all 76 links, and a full-text diff of the copy against DESIGN.md. Screenshots are in [`docs/judge-shots/`](judge-shots/).

**Hero claim check: PASS.** Both sites say the Kariba El Niño model's first alarm came in Jul 2023: 5 months before ZRA's cut (28 Dec 2023) and 8 months before load-shedding (11 Mar 2024). P(<20%) = 0.8 in Oct–Dec 2023. The record is 3 hits, 2 misses and 2 false alarms. The "now" model's Mar 2024 miss is disclosed. All of this matches §12. The 2027 numbers also match §12: P(<10%) = 36%, P(<20%) = 45%, median 31%.

Severity: **S1** costs points on a judging criterion and should be fixed before the 3 PM freeze · **S2** a judge will notice it · **S3** polish.
